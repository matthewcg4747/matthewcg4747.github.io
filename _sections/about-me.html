Hello! My name is Matthew and I am currently amidst a fellowship in Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia. I'd been flirting with the idea of writing more and I'm hoping this is my chance to get something out there. If there is anything that you have to know about me it's that I find myself consistently scattered and fractured. And this is not necessarily negative, at least for me it isn't. I'm hoping this website will allow me to compartmentalize my thoughts and feelings, a "tangible" outlet for me to express myself to the world if you may. I mean a yapper’s dream is to one day be given the chance to transcribe their thoughts instead of throwing it out into the void of conversation, not to say a conversation isn’t a good platform. Maybe this will be a chance to experience the validation of my thoughts and feelings, please interact with them. You might see me post a recap of an interesting week, an essay about a burning concept/idea that's been sitting with me, photographs that speak to me, etc etc. I think this will be a blog that ideally toes the line of formal and casual, (hopefully) intellectual and (probably) exceedingly trivial, and serious if not occasionally and sickishly sweet. More than anything I'm hoping to give those transient thoughts that I often find myself mourning a space to exist and thrive. Please enjoy!!
