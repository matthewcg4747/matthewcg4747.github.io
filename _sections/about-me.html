Hello, my name is Matthew and I have a goal for myself that I wanted to accomplish. I’ve never been a particularly strong writer, but I have felt like I always have different ideas bouncing around in my head that often die in the sands of memory. So in the hopes of eventually being the type of person who can eloquently express my thoughts and feelings I challenged myself to write 25 essays before I turn 25 and this is where I will post the ones that make it out the graveyard. I think my essays will ideally toe the line of formal and casual, (hopefully) intentional and (probably) exceedingly trivial, and serious if not occasionally and sickishly sweet. Please indulge my transient thoughts and in my occasional hypocrisy, but, most importantly, please enjoy. !
